# Limitations and Claim Boundaries

- All mission data and results are fictional, synthetic, unclassified, non-CUI,
  and non-authoritative.
- No AFDW, 844th Communications Group, Air Force, government, or enterprise
  inventory was accessed or modeled as a real network.
- No deployment, accreditation, ATO, CMMC assessment, SPRS score, GovCloud
  account validation, FIPS module validation, or government validation exists.
- The public core performs no live remediation, active scanning, connector call,
  key generation, signature, encryption, or operational response.
- Development metrics are not the sealed acceptance evaluation and cannot
  establish the numerical gates, TRL 5, operational relevance, or quantum advantage.
- The Python dependency audit reports no known vulnerabilities as of the frozen
  lock verification date; this is a point-in-time database result, not a warranty
  that undisclosed vulnerabilities do not exist.
- The GovCloud-ready Terraform reference, complete interoperability lab, million-
  observation scale run, Windows production packaging, and validator reports are
  not implemented in this foundation.
- Existing Qbit QAOA, simulator, scheduling, routing, allocation, naval,
  deployment, FIPS, energy, and performance assertions are quarantined from
  QSPARX claims until primary evidence and correctness/property tests exist.
